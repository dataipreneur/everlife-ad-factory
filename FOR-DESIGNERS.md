# Making Everlife ads with Claude: the 2-minute guide (2026-11-05)

You don't need to know anything technical. Here is the whole job.

## What you type
Any of these, in your own words:
- "Make 8 NAD+ ads" · "PT-141 statics please" · "Let's do Wolverine" · "What should I make today?" · "Make ads"

## What Claude asks (one screen)
1. **How many** (8 is the normal batch).
2. **Direction**: **🎲 Surprise me** (a different world for every ad) or one of three ideas Claude pitches for that product that
   day, for example *"Paper trail of spontaneity: a boarding pass, a dinner ticket, a memo"*. Pick one or press Enter for Surprise me.
3. **Who's in them**: a mix of product worlds and real people (normal), product only, or more people.
4. Only for products with two forms (PT-141, NAD+, GLP-1): which form, or let Claude alternate.

Then you see a table of the 8 ads (world, headline, lines) and one button: **Generate all 8 in both sizes (≈16 credits)**.

## What you get
- Every ad in **9:16 and 4:5**, finished with the Everlife logo, the proof row and the disclaimer, checked by Claude, on one
  contact sheet, in a folder that opens by itself: `~/Everlife-Creative/output/<Product>/<date>_batch-N/`.
- Ads that came back wrong (text too small, product covered) are re-made once inside the same budget before you see them.
- Everything is a draft until creative review and Jeremy's compliance sign-off.

## What you can say next
"Another 8 with new worlds" · "Variations of #3" · "Make #5 again, darker" · "Women only" · "All comic book" · "Use the boarding
pass idea for all of them" · "Why did you pick that headline?" Claude understands plain English; there are no commands to learn.

## Things Claude handles so you don't have to
Claims and wording (only approved lines), safe zones, text size for 50+, both sizes, the proof split, the disclaimer, the logo,
variety (no two ads alike, nothing repeated from last week), logging, posting finals to Ad Review.

## If something looks off
Say so in plain words ("the CTA is covering the bottle on #4"). Claude fixes it for free when it can, and tells you the cost when it
can't. Tools broken: Mitansh. Is this allowed to say: Jeremy.
