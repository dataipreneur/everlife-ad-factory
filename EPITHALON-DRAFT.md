## 5. Draft art direction

- **Audience / casting:** adults 40–65, all audiences, men and women in balance; real, ordinary, non-famous faces ("must not resemble
  any celebrity"); a different person in every ad; talent 45+ in video (Hook Engine default). Healthy, active, mature, not athletes
  or bodybuilders, never shirtless or flexing.
- **Story:** healthy aging and cellular support: calm, capable, engaged daytime life. Moments: a morning walk in sun, gardening,
  cooking with family, a pharmacy-counter pickup, a hike with a grown child, playing with grandchildren, reading at a sunny kitchen
  table, a pottery or yoga class, a desk by a window. Warm, sun-lit, candid (bible imagery rule); pharmacy framing as a trust cue (45+).
- **Never:** any sleep or night cue (see the contradiction above); bedrooms; before/after or younger-vs-older comparisons; mirrors
  used to judge the face/body; "anti-aging" or "reverse aging" wording or visuals; hospitals, IV drips, lab-result screens;
  needles of any kind; body-composition cues.
- **Look:** texture and depth behind the product (stone, linen, wood, soft daylight), never flat colour; no shadows/outlines on type.
  No palette limit (the women's pink/tan/beige rule applies only to GHK-Cu Cream and Glow Stack); suggested calm field colours
  (warm sand, soft sage-grey, morning-sky blue) pending the render's own colours.
- **Copy on every ad:** "Epithalon" big (one of the two largest texts; images never say "by Everlife"; videos carry "Epithalon by
  Everlife" in the header and first spoken line), one ✅ benefit, the proof row (Trustpilot rated 4.9 Excellent · Over 100,000
  happy customers), a ✅ CTA, the Everlife logo, the provisional disclaimer at the bottom. No offer line until the placeholder is filled.
- **Product:** the real render only, still and label-readable; pill held thumb + index or on an open palm beside the bottle (if a
  tablet/capsule) — exactly one pill on screen; vial is a still object, never thrown or dropped (if a vial). Form wording TBD.
- **Required options when built:** "✨ Ultra premium, product only" (`core/references/ultra-premium.md`) and "🧑‍💼 AI Avatar Video"
  (`core/references/ai-avatar-video.md`: casting as above, hero = the real form, banned settings = bedrooms/night/clinical).
