## What all eight have in common (keep these)
- **Frame 0 is already action** (wall smash, banner rip, bottle mid-air, gauge crash, glow being sipped) with a real sound at 0.0 s.
- **The problem is said in the first ~1–2 s**, in plain words, with no ages ("Low growth hormone?", "Training hard but still feeling flat?",
  "Your body still makes growth hormone. Just less of it."). Don't reuse the exact same opener twice in a row.
- **A clear mini-story**: problem → product name "by Everlife" by ~3 s → what it does (each claim on its own approved line or ingredient) →
  how it's taken / doctor prescribed → offer → urgent CTA. Never cryptic fragments (`video-team-rules.md`).
- **One generation per video** where possible (start still → Seedance 2.5 reference-to-video with the real renders as @images).
- **Captions on every video** (bible Standards 02), inside the 9:16 safe zone, plus the peptide list on every stack video
  (Wolverine, Spartan: at least on the end card; bible 2026-11-03).
- **Finishing (0 credits) does the rest**: Everlife logo top; product name on screen from frame 0 but NEVER over a face (header only
  while no face is in the top third, else a slim name line); the bible offer on the end-card button ("Save up to N%", never "Get Started
  Now"); the animated heartbeat "DOUBLE STRENGTH" badge on SGHK 1000; nothing ever covers the product, label or face; free fixes before
  any paid re-take.
