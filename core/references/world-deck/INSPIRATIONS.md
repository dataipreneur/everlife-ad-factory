# Inspiration bank: the directions Claude pitches before a batch (2026-11-05)

Mitansh: "Claude should recommend based on the product what styles to make, with Surprise me as 1st always, but it should give
super cool inspirations." So the image question's second item is **Direction**: "🎲 Surprise me (Recommended)" first, then
THREE directions Claude writes for THIS product, THIS batch: a name, one sentence of the idea, the 3–5 worlds it is built from,
and the twist that makes it this product's. Pick them from the seeds below, mutate them (room, object, era, material), never
offer the same three twice in a row (the world ledger shows what ran), and invent one new direction per batch when you can.
A chosen direction goes to the dealer as `--prefer <world ids>` (those worlds are guaranteed in the batch); the remaining slots
stay random so the batch is still a mix.

## How to write a direction (the three options in the question)
Label: `<Name> · <3–4 world names>`. Description: one sentence of the idea for THIS product + the twist + "≈2 cr/ad, both sizes".
Example (PT-141): **Paper trail of spontaneity · boarding pass, order ticket, memo, poll** — "A night that leaves a paper trail:
a boarding pass FROM calendars TO moments, a dinner ticket that reads 'Tonight's order: no plan', a memo to the planner.
Warm paper, daylight, product on every page. ≈2 cr/ad, both sizes."
