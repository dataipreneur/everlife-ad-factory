# Variation: intimacy-tasteful (intimate-wellness products)

Products: PT-141, Lovara, Spark, Titan-Rx. Source: bible product pages ("Keep it tasteful, product imagery led, no bedroom
scenes"; PT-141 "video framing stays shoulder-up"), gates §3.

- **Never a bed, bedroom, mattress, pillow scene, lingerie, body shot, couple in bed, reclining person.** The dealer drops worlds
  flagged `not-intimacy` and the bed-adjacent ones (drop-the-day, lamp-reader, capsule-sofa, big-hug, mascot).
- People: **clothed, candid, 30–55, shoulder-up or waist-up, daylight** rooms (kitchen, hallway, terrace, café, car, garden,
  bathroom counter). A couple is fine shoulder-up, laughing, never touching in bed-like framing.
- Product-led: the spray / tablet / capsule is the hero in most slots; the mood is warm and a little knowing, never clinical, never crude.
- Words: no "arousal" in any form (PT-141 Hard NO), no "ED", "performance", timing promises ("tonight", "in minutes"), no
  pronoun questions. Mechanism first ("supports brain-based desire").
- PT-141 spray is held, never sprayed on camera; tablet and spray never in the same ad (see `two-forms`).
