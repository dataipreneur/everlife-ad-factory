# 📋 Team video rules (Mitansh, 2026-11-03, HARD, every product skill, every teammate)

> Mitansh, 2026-11-03: "this should be across all skills, not just my local system!" These came out of the Wolverine,
> Sermorelin GHK and Restora-Z video sessions of 2026-11-03. The gates (`core/claude/everlife-gates.md` §5) point here.

## 1. Scripts must make sense (every video script)
- Write a **clear mini-story a stranger follows on the first listen**: problem → why it happens → what the product is → what it
  does (plain words, approved claims only) → how it's taken / trust → offer → CTA. Full, connected sentences; each line leads to the next.
- **Intense ≠ fragmented.** No cryptic shorthand chains ("One tablet. Three weapons." / "Citrulline: blood flow and pump.") —
  Mitansh called that script "0 sense… trash" while praising the visuals.
- The picture matches the words (show the tablet when the line says "this tablet").
- The problem is said in the first ~1–2 s (a pronoun-free question works: "Training hard but still feeling flat?").
- End on an urgent, specific spoken CTA that **leads with the approved offer** ("Save up to 47%. Don't sit on this!"). "Get Started
  Now" is banned (bible sync 2026-11-03); where the offer is a `[PLACEHOLDER]`, use "Ships Free From Pharmacy". No fake deadlines or scarcity.
- **No specific ages or age numbers** in video scripts, spoken lines or captions ("45", "after 35", "forty-five").
  Say "with age" / "every birthday" if needed. (Priestley 2026-11-03: videos only; bible-approved image headlines with an age,
  e.g. "Thrive After 35", stay usable.)
- Read it aloud before showing it; if a line needs explaining, rewrite it.

## 2. Show scripts in the chat, then stop
- Present a script (or anything the user must read) as plain chat text and **end the turn** with one line ("Say go, or tell me
  what to change"). Never open an AskUserQuestion popup in the same turn: it covers the script.
- Popups are only for quick choices that need no reading.

## 3. No paid re-take without an explicit "go"
- "Fix it" / "make it slower" is NOT approval to spend. When a fix costs credits, give the **free fix first** (ffmpeg trims,
  speed changes on non-speech parts, re-finishing, overlays) and the paid option with its cost, then wait for "go".
- Never speed up talking (lip-synced speech stays at its natural pace); speed up only throws, actions and pauses.

## 4. Music for men's videos
- Every men's video gets heavy, masculine music ducked under the voice (Creatify `composer_generate_music`, Lyria, 0.8 each),
  with the first hit on the hook's sound at 0.0 s.
- **Picked:** trap-metal (half-time trap drums, booming 808s, a crushing detuned riff) and the hip-hop / hard-rock walk-out.
  **Rejected:** taiko / tribal war-drums only. Offer 2 contrasting takes when the user doesn't like a track.
