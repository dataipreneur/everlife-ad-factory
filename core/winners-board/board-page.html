The board fills as the team rates finished work. Each rating becomes a card here with a preview of the ad, sorted into Winners, Active and Retired.

  1. After a video or an image batch, the operator's Claude asks one question per ad (Winner / Good / Weak / Kill) and writes it here with its recipe and a preview.
  2. Reviewers open "To review", rate each ad and leave feedback. Their majority decides over the operator's rating.
  3. When an ad has run, add its Meta results (CTR, CPA, hook rate). They decide over everything else.
