# Everlife Hook Bank (v1, 2026-10-30)

Starting points, not scripts. Every hook still passes the HOOK-ENGINE contract, the gates and the product's skill rules. Vary
the room, object, cast and angle every time. "Bridge" = which kind of bible line it leads into; the actual wording always comes
from the bible.

## Restora-Z (sleep · adults 45–65 · bottle + exactly one capsule)
Urgency = a vibrating twin-bell alarm clock in the ad's palette (never red flashing/rings). No readable clock digits (use analog hands).
1. SAME-FRAME FLIP · Sheep stampede, then silence. 3 am kitchen overrun by a herd of sheep → same frame, empty and calm, bottle on the counter. Sound: hooves + bleat at 0.0 s.
2. IMPACT · Alarm clock jumps off nightstand. The twin-bell clock rattles off the edge; a hand catches it mid-air. Sound: bell clatter.
3. POV · From inside the nightstand drawer. The drawer yanks open; a wide-awake face looks in; a hand reaches past to the bottle.
4. SOUND-FIRST · Thunderous snore, snap zoom. On a couch/armchair, a partner presses a cushion over their own ears. Bridge: waking through the night.
5. MISDIRECTION · Nature-doc whisper on insomnia. A hushed documentary narrator observes "a wide-awake adult in its natural habitat" at 3 am; swerve to the bottle.
6. ABSURD ESCALATION · Pillow flipping goes pro. Flip to the cool side, again, faster, until the pillow spins like pizza dough; it lands, calm.
7. SPEED · Clock hands whirl, eyes stay open. Locked overhead; analog clock hands spin a full night in 1 s; the eyes never close.
8. IMPACT · Pharmacy bag thud at dawn. The bag lands on the doormat; the bottle slides out to camera. Bridge: ships free from the pharmacy.
9. STREET · "How many wake-ups last night?" (MS Interview). Third-person answers, ending on the bottle.
10. MS Random Object Mic · the pillow (or the alarm clock) becomes the mic for a serious explanation.
11. MS Product Dodge · a bottle flies across the living room, they duck, come up holding it.
12. CONTRAST SPLIT · Same room, two halves: left side, wide awake with the lamp on; right side, dark and peaceful. Not a body comparison.
