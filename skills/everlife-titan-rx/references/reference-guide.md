Rules of thumb:
- Mike's rule: **one product, one capsule, one promise**, one plain line that explains it; the formula named together or not at all.
- Ray's rule: big type, a man who looks 40–65, nothing crude; if the pin's mood is "bedroom", take only its type scale.
- Never reuse the same pin for two slots in one batch; never the same framework twice unless the batch is bigger than 8.
