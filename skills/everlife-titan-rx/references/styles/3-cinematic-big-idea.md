# Category 3 — Cinematic Big Idea

Library folders: Movie-Peg · Wild-Creative/Surreal · Retro · New Year · Billboard (boards 1, 3, 4, 5, 6).
Why it works: pattern interrupts for a product people don't expect to see advertised; keep it strong and tasteful, never suggestive.

## 3A — Forged metaphor
- **Looks like:** a Seedance macro: chalk bursting off a barbell in slow motion; a steel garage door rolling up on dawn light; a rope slam frozen mid-air. The product arrives in the final beat, label to camera. One a bible reference line line in finishing ("When It’s Go Time, Be Ready").
- **Guardrails:** the metaphor is about daily readiness and strength, never about bodies or body parts; no fire, no lightning, no innuendo objects.
- **Finishing:** `default` (house look) or `editorial`.

## 3B — Comedic contrast
- **Looks like:** a fridge covered in "plan around it" sticky notes and alarms vs the same fridge with one note: the bottle beside it ("Once-Daily, No Timing Required").
- **Guardrails:** humour about schedules, never about anyone's performance.

## 3C — Animated circulation
- **Looks like:** a stylised, abstract flow of light (not anatomical) through a steel-and-chrome graphic, landing on the capsule; "Supports Blood Flow When It Counts".
- **Guardrails:** no anatomy, no body outline below the shoulders, no ingredient-by-ingredient effects.

## 3D — Retro film
- **Looks like:** 70s film grain, a man and his wife 50–65 laughing at a diner booth (clothed, daytime), the product on the table.

## 3E — Seasonal
- **Looks like:** Father's Day / New Year training resolution / anniversary dinner framing with the product; no bedrooms, no lingerie, no gifts that imply intimacy.
