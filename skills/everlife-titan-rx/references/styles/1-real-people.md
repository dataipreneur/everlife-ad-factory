# Category 1 — Real People Talking

Library folders: UGC · Talking Head Avatar · AI Avatar · Conversational · Podcast · Voice Over (boards 1, 2, 4, 6).
Why it works: the highest trust for a 40–65 buyer; a real, strong man naming the goal out loud. For Titan-Rx the face is the bible's
talent (rugged, strong, unmistakably masculine, a hardcore-training feel, 40–65), but the talk stays about the product (daily, no
timing, pharmacy), never about results, his partner or his private life.

**Engine: Marketing Studio UGC** (`--mode ugc --product_ids @<skill>/assets/product_ids.json --duration 15 --resolution 720p --aspect_ratio 9:16 --generate-audio true`), 75 credits. Needs Titan-Rx registered first (not done yet). Never Seedance or still-image lip-sync for these.

Universal prompt lines: "ultra-realistic creator UGC, real handheld phone shake, real skin texture, real room audio, no beauty filter; the filming phone is NEVER visible; label readable and never mirrored; fully clothed (training tee or hoodie), head-and-shoulders framing; no bare torso, no bed or bedroom in frame; nobody says 'I' or 'my'; say 'Titan-Rx' as 'TIE-tan R-X'; keep the bottom 25% free of faces."

---

## 1A — Creator to camera
- **Looks like:** a man 45–60 between sets in a garage gym or leaning on his truck after training, talking to his phone, the bottle in hand by the cheek.
- **Beats:** 0–2 a pattern-interrupt action (drops a kettlebell, chalk puffs) → 2–6 "Titan-Rx by Ever-life. Once-daily, no timing required." → 6–10 product up + ONE ✅ line ("Supports blood flow when it counts.") → 10–12 "Doctor prescribed. Ships free from the pharmacy."
- **Finishing:** `tiktok`. **Guardrails:** describe the product, never his results or his partner; no "ED".

## 1B — Gym-buddy to buddy
- **Looks like:** two men 45–65 racking plates; A asks what the black bottle is; B: "Titan-Rx. Once-daily, three-in-one. Doctor prescribed."
- **Guardrails:** Rx never shared; no locker-room nudity; nothing about anyone's nights; the off-camera voice never testifies.
- **Finishing:** `default` (house look).

## 1C — Podcast set
- **Looks like:** two hosts 45–60 at a dark-wood podcast desk, the product on the desk.
- **Beats:** the host's take ("Daily support beats planning around a pill") → each host gives ONE ✅ benefit → product on desk.
- **Finishing:** `editorial`. **Guardrails:** no "studies show" for the product; no "ED"; no competitor names.

## 1D — Voice-over lifestyle
- **Looks like:** a calm narrator over 3–4 candid shots of one man (dawn garage door, chalked hands, coffee in the kitchen, a laugh at a terrace dinner), benefit chips added in finishing.
- **Finishing:** `default` (house look) or `editorial`.
