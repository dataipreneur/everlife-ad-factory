# Category 4 — Product as Hero  (highest fit: tasteful, product-led)

Library folders: Product-as-Hero · Product Highlight · Pill-Product Focused · Minimalist · Polaroid (boards 3, 4, 5).
Why it works: the intimate-wellness gate is product-led; the matte black + chrome Titan-Rx bottle and the mirror-chrome capsule are distinctive on their own.

## 4A — Forged still life
- **Looks like:** one capsule (hero, in front) and the bottle on black stone, gunmetal or brushed steel, one hard light, the red chevron the only colour. Bold sans a bible reference line line.
- Static: batch F12. Video: Seedance slow push-in, 480p is enough.

## 4B — Capsule macro
- **Looks like:** the single chrome capsule on a chalked barbell sleeve, an iron plate or dark oak, the bottle behind, label sharp. Never two capsules.
- Static: batch F2/F11. Video: Seedance macro orbit (watch the chrome reflections in QA).

## 4C — Minimal bold
- **Looks like:** one product, one colour field (black, gunmetal, concrete, sand), one huge headline, three short lines. The §2b type menu does the variety.

## 4D — Ad in a frame
- **Looks like:** the ad as a poster on a garage-gym wall or a card on a workbench (never a nightstand).

## 4E — Morning-routine ASMR
- **Looks like:** hands set the bottle down on a kitchen counter or gym bench among everyday objects (watch, keys, a coffee, chalk bag); a garage door rolls; a towel drops. Shoulder-up if a face appears.
- **Guardrails:** the product is placed, never swallowed on camera; one capsule only.
