# Category 6 — Lifestyle & Identity

Library folders: Female Glow Up · Life Cell · Sports · Elite/Celebrity Secret · Winners (boards 1, 2, 3, 5).
Why it works: the 40–65 buyer sees himself: a strong man who still trains hard and wants daily readiness without planning. Always clothed, shoulder-up/waist-up, never a bed.

## 6A — Iron and dawn
- **Looks like:** a rugged man 45–65 in a hardcore gym or garage gym at dawn (chalk, plates, rope), training tee or hoodie, candid; the product on the bench. "Supports Daily Performance."
- **Guardrails:** no bare torso, no flexing, no mirror selfies; fit, not a bodybuilder caricature.
- **Finishing:** `default` (house look) or `tiktok`.

## 6B — Gym to dinner
- **Looks like:** the same man later: a terrace dinner with his wife 45–60, both laughing, clothed, golden hour; the product never at the table, it closes the ad on a counter. "Be Ready When She Is Ready."
- **Guardrails:** the evening is dinner, a drive, a walk; it never arrives at a bedroom. Shoulder-up.

## 6C — Confidence + control
- **Looks like:** a man 50–65 in a workshop or on a trail, calm and certain, mid-task; the product on a workbench. "Encourages Confidence + Control."
- **Guardrails:** identity, not transformation; no before/after, no body commentary, no timing numbers.

## 6D — Everyday ready
- **Looks like:** a morning routine: garage door up, coffee, keys, the bottle on the counter, out the door to train. "Supports Everyday Readiness."
- **Finishing:** `editorial`.
