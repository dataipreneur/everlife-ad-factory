# Persona critique — the target buyer reads the ad before Mitansh does

Run this on EVERY concept (image or video) after writing it and BEFORE the approval question. It is free: no tools, no credits.
Write the critique in the persona's voice, first person, 3–5 lines, timestamped for video or top-to-bottom for a static.
Then list the fixes you made because of it. Show the critique under the concept so Mitansh sees what Maria and Ron saw.

## Persona 1 — "Maria", 41 (the bible's core demo: the 40-year-old mom)
Two kids, a job, snacks at 9 pm. Has tried every diet; distrusts anything that looks like a miracle or a body ad, and hates surprise
pricing. What Maria punishes: before/after or body talk ("I don't need to be shamed"), a price that isn't clear, needles, anything
she'd hide from her kids on the couch, drug-brand look-alikes.

## Persona 2 — "Ron", 56
Doctor told him to watch his weight; wants a provider, not an influencer. What Ron punishes: hype words, numbers he can't trust
("20%?"), disease words, no clear next step.

## The rubric (score each 1–5; anything under 4 gets fixed before approval)
1. **Clear in 2 seconds:** what it is (one form), who it's for, the one promise.
2. **Plain English:** every claim word is explained by the scene or a bible line; no jargon stack.
3. **Not scammy:** real product, real pharmacy proof, no cure words, no timing numbers, no needles.
4. **Looks like me:** an everyday adult and a calm kitchen, not a body.
5. **Worth a click:** the end card answers "what do I do now" (a bible CTA) and "what's it cost" (the price line, Save Up to 83%).

Write the critique honestly. If Maria or Ron wouldn't click, say so and fix the concept before showing it.
