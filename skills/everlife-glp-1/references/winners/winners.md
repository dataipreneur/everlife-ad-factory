## How to use these cards
- Take **layout, format, staging, palette and type**. Take **every word** from `product-bible.md` (✅ lines; ⚠ only with the user's
  OK). These ads carry the bible's GLP-1 disclaimer, but they lean on the weight-loss outcome lines Priestley held for Jeremy.

## What they have in common
1. **Both vials side by side** (navy compounded semaglutide + green compounded tirzepatide), large, front and centre.
2. **"GLP-1 + GIP"** as a kicker (tirzepatide is GLP-1 + GIP). The bible's umbrella term is "GLP-1": use "GLP-1" in headers.
3. **Crossed-out alternatives:** a Notes checklist with "donuts? chips? what's for dinner?" struck through (S186), or "Calorie apps
   / Snack swaps / Willpower" struck through (S193). This is the F1 / F3 format.
4. **Happy, active, clothed people** (a couple playing pickleball, a couple in a bright kitchen): lifestyle, not bodies.
5. **Three check-mark benefits** and a proof row: "4.9 Excellent on Trustpilot · Same-day approval · Ships overnight".
