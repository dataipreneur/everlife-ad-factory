# Category 4 — Product as Hero

Library folders: Product-as-Hero · Product Highlight · Pill-Product Focused · Minimalist · Polaroid (boards 3, 4, 5).
Why it works: the navy or green vial is a distinctive object; the Premium DTC codes (`13_Premium_DTC`) suit them.

## 4A — Still life
- **Looks like:** the vial on stone, linen or light wood in daylight, hard side light, one long shadow; or on walnut for
  the quiet variant. Sentence-case Tier A line. Static: batch F12 / F14. Video: Seedance slow push-in, 480p.
- **Guardrails:** the vial is a still object; no syringe, pen, dropper or droplets; no capsules.

## 4B — Vial macro
- **Looks like:** a macro of the navy or green label and crimp with condensation.
- Static: batch F2 / F11. Video: Seedance macro orbit.

## 4C — Minimal bold
- **Looks like:** one product, one colour field (cream, stone, linen; the vial's navy or green as the accent), one huge headline, up to three quick-hit lines.

## 4D — Ad in a frame
- **Looks like:** the ad as a poster on a kitchen wall or a card on the fridge door.

## 4E — Weekly plan
- **Looks like:** hands set out a calendar, a water bottle and keys on a kitchen table, the product set down last. The product is placed, never used.
