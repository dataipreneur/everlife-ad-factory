## Rules for using a review (style 2B, `--layout quote`)
1. **Verbatim only.** You may shorten a quote ONLY by cutting whole sentences, marking the cut with "…"; never change words, spelling, order or meaning, and never stitch sentences from different reviews.
2. **Name format:** first name + last initial as shown on Trustpilot (e.g. "Mike Z."), plus "Trustpilot". Proof line stays "Trustpilot rated 4.9 Excellent".
3. **Never put a reviewer's name next to an AI-generated face** (it would imply that person is the reviewer). Pair quotes with product shots, hands, or scenes with no identifiable face.
4. Testimonials are claims: the creative bible's rules still apply (no disease words, no "cure", no superlatives about the product, no safety claims, no comparisons to named drugs). The disclaimer ("Individual results may vary") must be present (the layouts add it).
5. **Every review ad goes to Jeremy** with the source URL and the review date in the SESSION.md hand-off.
