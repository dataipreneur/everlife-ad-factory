## 3A — Movie-peg (pop-culture riff, no IP)
- **Looks like:** a cinematic homage to a famous scene *without using names, logos, characters or quotes from the film*. Proven library example: the blue-pill choice; our capsule is literally blue.
- **Ideas:** "Two capsules, one choice": a hand offers an open palm with the ONE Restora-Z capsule in green-code rain → a slow-mo catch → a morning sunrise. A heist-film "the plan" whiteboard of a 3 a.m. brain (no text on the board; arrows only). A space-launch countdown to "lights out" (no numbers on screen; VO counts).
- **Beats:** 0–2 the iconic visual → 2–8 a dramatic slow-mo product reveal → 8–12 a calm payoff.
- **Finishing:** `editorial` or `house`.
- **Guardrails:** no film titles, actor likenesses, logos or quotes. A *non-claim* hook line ("Two capsules. One choice." / "Pick your night.") may be spoken or shown as a caption; **headers, benefits and claims still come only from the bible**.
- **Starter concept:** "The choice": a narrator: "Another night of tossing and turning… or one prescription capsule." Header *Get Your 3-in-1 Sleep Solution*.
