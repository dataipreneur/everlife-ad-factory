## 6A — Active 50+ mornings
- **Looks like:** a 55–65 person starting the day with ease: an early tennis rally, a dawn swim, a dog walk, gardening. The energy comes from *being rested*, not from muscles.
- **Copy:** benefit lines like *Wake Up Energized Without Grogginess*, *Wake Up Refreshed* (check the copy log).
- **Engine:** Marketing Studio (a realistic person, light talking) or GPT Image 2.5 static.
- **Finishing:** `tiktok` / `standard`.

## 6B — "Night shift" identity
- **Looks like:** an identity hook: nurses, truckers, new grandparents, frequent travellers who struggle to switch off. A short, respectful slice of life, then the product.
- **Guardrails:** no shift-work sleep-disorder or jet-lag claims; the pain point stays a bible one ("Struggling to relax at night", "Can't fall or stay asleep").
- **Engine:** Marketing Studio.

## 6C — Couple wellness
- **Looks like:** a couple in their late 50s with a calm evening ritual (tea, book, lights out) → both up early for a sunrise walk.
- **Guardrails:** no bedroom intimacy cues; each has their OWN prescription (no sharing).
- **Engine:** Marketing Studio or a GPT Image 2.5 static (`standard`).
