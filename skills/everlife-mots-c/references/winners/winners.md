## How to use them (read before every batch)
- **Take:** layout, structure, staging, palette, type, the format of the hook.
- **Swap:** the other product → the **MOTS-c vial** (lemon-yellow label, a still object, never with a needle); the other name →
  **"MOTS-C"** big.
- **Words:** only from `../product-bible.md` (✅ lines; "Supports Glucose Metabolism" is ⚠ for Jeremy).
- **MOTS-c is in the bible's metabolic / weight / body-composition category: the strictest rules.** Never copy, from any of
  them:
  - weight-loss lines ("Up to 20% weight loss"), scales, tape measures, waistlines, physique or midriff shots;
  - "Improved insulin sensitivity", or any disease word;
  - "Boosts…", "Reduces fatigue…", "DNA repair" (held for NAD+ too);
  - made-up ingredient pills;
  - "GHK-Cu", "needle-free", old ratings (4.7 / 4.8), $ prices;
  - their offer badges or numbers (MOTS-c's only offer is "Save up to 40%").
