# MOTS-c art direction (2026-10-30; no bible creative direction yet, global rules only)

**Who:** active adults 30–55, men and women, who train for energy and endurance: runners, cyclists, hikers, open-water swimmers,
rowers, someone training for a first half-marathon. Fit, real, mid-effort or just after; ordinary non-famous faces.

**The picture is energy and distance, never physique:** a trail at dawn, a bike on a quiet road, a rowing machine in morning light,
a pool lane, a finish-line chute, a kitchen before a long run. Shoulder-up or full-body in motion is fine; **no shirtless physique
shots, no abs, no waistlines, no scales, no tape measures, no "after" bodies, no mirrors** (body-composition Hard NOs).

**Palette:** the vial's lemon yellow as the accent against charcoal, slate, white, sky blue, stone; or warm dawn light. No neon
"fat-burner" look.

**Type:** clean, confident sans or a modern serif; nothing that looks like a supplement-shop shred label.

**Never:** needles, syringes, pens or injecting (the vial is a still object); disease words (diabetes, blood sugar control, insulin
resistance); weight or fat words; "boost", "burn", "melt", "ripped", "shred".
