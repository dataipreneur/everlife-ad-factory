# Persona critique — the target buyer reads the ad before Mitansh does

Run this on EVERY concept (image or video) after writing it and BEFORE the approval question. It is free: no tools, no credits.
Write the critique in the persona's voice, first person, 3–5 lines, timestamped for video or top-to-bottom for a static.
Then list the fixes you made because of it. Show the critique under the concept so Mitansh sees what Dan and Carla saw.

## Persona 1 — "Distance Dan", 48
Runs four mornings a week, a half-marathon each spring; his energy fades by the last third. Has tried every gel and pre-workout; hates
"fat-burner" ads. Will not click anything that looks like a supplement shop or promises a body.
What Dan punishes: weight or body talk ("this isn't about my belly"), jargon with no plain line ("mitochondrial" needs "the cell's
energy" next to it), hype speed ("in days"), needles.

## Persona 2 — "Cycling Carla", 44
Rides 100 miles a week, careful with anything that sounds like a diet product or a blood-sugar drug.
What Carla punishes: "metabolism" next to weight or food; disease words; a shirtless or physique image; anything neon.

## The rubric (score each 1–5; anything under 4 gets fixed before approval)
1. **Clear in 2 seconds:** what it is (one form), who it's for, the one promise.
2. **Plain English:** every claim word is explained by the scene or a bible line; no jargon stack.
3. **Not scammy:** real product, real pharmacy proof, no cure words, no timing numbers, no needles.
4. **Looks like me:** an active adult in motion, not a physique.
5. **Worth a click:** the end card answers "what do I do now" (a ✅ CTA; "Save up to 40%" where the slot carries the offer).

Write the critique honestly. If Dan or Carla wouldn't click, say so and fix the concept before showing it.
