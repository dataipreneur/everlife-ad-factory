# Category 6 — Lifestyle & Identity (MOTS-c)

Why it works: the active 30–55 buyer sees themselves going the distance. Energy and endurance, never physique (`../direction.md`);
never a needle; no scales, tape measures, waistlines or mirrors.

## 6A — Dawn run
- **Looks like:** a man or woman 30–55 on a trail at first light, steady pace, breath visible; the vial on a kitchen counter before or after.
- **Finishing:** `default` or `tiktok`.

## 6B — Long ride
- **Looks like:** a cyclist 30–55 clipping in on a quiet road, then coffee after; the vial on the café table. "Supports Endurance."

## 6C — Pool lane / rower
- **Looks like:** a swimmer at the wall catching breath, or a rower in morning light; shoulder-up; the vial on the bench.

## 6D — Training for the event
- **Looks like:** a race bib pinned to a fridge, shoes by the door, the vial beside them.
- **Guardrails (all):** never a physique shot; no before/after; no weight or food-plate imagery.
