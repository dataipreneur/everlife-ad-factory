# Category 3 — Cinematic Big Idea

Library folders: Movie-Peg · Wild-Creative/Surreal · Retro · New Year · Billboard (boards 1, 3, 4, 5, 6).
Why it works: pattern interrupts for a product people don't expect to see advertised; keep it witty and tasteful, never suggestive.

## 3A — Switch / match / spark metaphor
- **Looks like:** a Seedance macro: a vintage light switch flipped on in a sunlit hallway; a match striking in slow motion; a compass needle swinging to a point. The product arrives in the final beat, label to camera. One Tier A line in finishing ("Awaken the Desire Response.").
- **Guardrails:** the metaphor is about desire *signaling*, never about bodies; no flames near people.
- **Finishing:** `default` (house look) or `editorial`.

## 3B — Comedic contrast
- **Looks like:** a wall calendar with "date night" scribbled every second Friday vs a torn-off page and a spontaneous Tuesday ("Made for Moments, Not Calendars").
- **Guardrails:** humour about schedules, never about anyone's performance.

## 3C — Animated brain-signal
- **Looks like:** a stylised animation of a signal travelling brain → body outline (abstract, not anatomical), landing on the product.
- **Guardrails:** no receptor science, no anatomy below the shoulders.

## 3D — Retro film
- **Looks like:** 70s film grain, a couple laughing at a diner booth (clothed, daytime), the product on the table.

## 3E — Seasonal
- **Looks like:** Valentine's / anniversary / summer-evening framing with the product; no bedrooms, no lingerie, no gifts that imply intimacy.
