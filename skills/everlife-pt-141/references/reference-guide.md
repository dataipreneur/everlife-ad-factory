# Which reference to use when (PT-141)

The reference library is the SHARED one, `core/references/static-refs/` (12 folders, mostly sleep and wellness brands; overview in
`OVERVIEW.jpg`). It teaches **layout, type scale, product staging and mood**, never words or scenes. Mitansh (2026-10-28): keep these
same references; know what to use when. Pick ONE pin per slot, look at it, and borrow only its structure.

| PT-141 angle (bible) | Best frameworks | In the folder, pick the pin that is… | Avoid in that folder |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The wanting, not the ability** — "It Was Never Just Physical", "More Than Blood Flow", "Desire Starts in the Brain" | F2 Bold Claim · F8 Question · F1 Old way vs New way | the big-type, one-idea pins: a single serif or grotesque line over calm space, product lower third (02: the "Sleep is self care" serif pin; 08: the huge "Did you know?" style, off-white, a hand holding the product) | the busy "stacked benefits + badges" pins |
| **Unplanned night** — "Some Nights Aren't Planned.", "Made for Moments, Not Calendars", "Desire Support on Your Schedule" | F10 Sticky Note · F3 Phone UI · F11 Visual Metaphor · F13 Portrait + line | 10: the note-on-an-object pins; 03: the calendar/reminder/toggle UI pins (ours shows an unplanned Tuesday, not an alarm); 11: the single-object metaphor pins (switch, button, "zoom in for a sign" hand) | anything with a bed (05, most of 04) |
| **Your body changed** — "Your Body Changed. Your Desire Doesn't Have to Disappear." | F13 Portrait + line · F12 Brand Mood · F9 Reasons Why | 12: the candid person + product pins (the eye-mask portrait's framing, not its bed); 09: the "3 reasons" numbered list with one product hero | the "poor sleep shortens your life" fear pins |
| **Non-hormonal / brain-based explained plainly** | F4 Features & Benefits · F9 Reasons Why · F1 Old way vs New way | 04: the three-icon-row pins with ONE product and lots of air (the "Breathe better" / "Less tossing" quiet ones); 01: the two-column myth/fact pins | 04's neon/3D pins; 05 entirely (banned) |
| **Trust (doctor prescribed, pharmacy, 100,000+)** | F2 · F6 Offer · F12 | 06: the clean percent-off pins (never $); 12: the pharmacy-feel still lifes | 07 until reviews exist |
| **Women-only audience** | any of the above | the blush / cream / soft-light pins in 02, 08, 12 | the navy-chrome and neon pins; anything orange or green |

Rules of thumb:
- Dave's rule: **one form per ad** (spray or sublingual), one promise, one plain line that explains the promise.
- Maya's rule: if the pin's mood is "bedroom", take only its type scale and leave the scene.
- Never reuse the same pin for two slots in one batch; never the same framework twice unless the batch is bigger than 8.
- Our own past PT-141 ads are in `team-refs/` (layout only; their faults are listed there).
