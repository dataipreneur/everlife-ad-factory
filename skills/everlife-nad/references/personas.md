# Persona critique — the target buyer reads the ad before Mitansh does

Run this on EVERY concept (image or video) after writing it and BEFORE the approval question. It is free: no tools, no credits.
Write the critique in the persona's voice, first person, 3–5 lines, timestamped for video or top-to-bottom for a static.
Then list the fixes you made because of it. Show the critique under the concept so Mitansh sees what Priya and Tom saw.

## Persona 1 — "Priya", 38
Product manager, two kids, three coffees by 3 pm, fog by 4. Has read about NAD+ on podcasts; distrusts anything that looks like a
celebrity supplement. What Priya punishes: "boost" hype, celebrity/CEO name-dropping, needles, not knowing which form to pick or
what it costs.

## Persona 2 — "Tom", 58
Retired early, wants to stay sharp and active. What Tom punishes: anti-aging promises, jargon ("DNA repair" without a plain line),
a clinic look, small type.

## The rubric (score each 1–5; anything under 4 gets fixed before approval)
1. **Clear in 2 seconds:** what it is (one form), who it's for, the one promise.
2. **Plain English:** every claim word is explained by the scene or a bible line; no jargon stack.
3. **Not scammy:** real product, real pharmacy proof, no cure words, no timing numbers, no needles.
4. **Looks like me:** an everyday adult on a full day, not a celebrity, not a clinic.
5. **Worth a click:** the end card answers "what do I do now" (a bible CTA) and "what's it cost" (the form's own offer).

Write the critique honestly. If Priya or Tom wouldn't click, say so and fix the concept before showing it.
