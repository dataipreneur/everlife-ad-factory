# Category 4 — Product as Hero

Library folders: Product-as-Hero · Product Highlight · Pill-Product Focused · Minimalist · Polaroid (boards 3, 4, 5).
Why it works: the green bottle with one tablet, or the green vial, is a distinctive object; the Premium DTC codes (`13_Premium_DTC`) suit them.

## 4A — Still life
- **Looks like:** the product on a white stone counter or a desk by a window, hard side light, one long shadow; or on walnut for
  the quiet variant. Sentence-case a bible reference line line. Static: batch F12 / F14. Video: Seedance slow push-in, 480p.
- **Guardrails:** one form per ad (both only for "lead with the choice"); exactly one tablet; the vial is a still object; no syringe,
  pen, dropper or droplets; no capsules.

## 4B — Tablet / vial macro
- **Looks like:** a macro of the green tablet beside its bottle, or the vial's green label and silver crimp.
- Static: batch F2 / F11. Video: Seedance macro orbit.

## 4C — Minimal bold
- **Looks like:** one product, one colour field (white, soft grey, charcoal; the product's green as the accent), one huge headline, up to three quick-hit lines.

## 4D — Ad in a frame
- **Looks like:** the ad as a poster in an office kitchen or a card on a desk pinboard.

## 4E — Morning bag
- **Looks like:** hands pack a work bag on a kitchen table: laptop, keys, water bottle, the product set down last. The product is placed, never used.
