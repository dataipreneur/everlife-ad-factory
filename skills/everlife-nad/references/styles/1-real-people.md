# Category 1 — Real People Talking (Marketing Studio UGC, ~75 cr / 15 s)

Library folders: UGC · Talking Head Avatar · Conversational · Podcast · Voice Over (boards 2, 4, 6).
Why it works: brain fog and burnout are a conversation people already have at work and at home.

## 1A — Creator to camera (morning or desk)
- **Looks like:** a man or woman 30–65 at a kitchen table or desk, phone propped on a mug, talking plainly. The product
  sits in frame; never used. One ✅ line, e.g. "Less Fog. More Focus."
- **Lines:** ≤ 35 words, one angle, one quick-hit line, pronoun-free, no "I" (the creator talks about focus and energy, not about themselves).

## 1B — Colleague / friend POV
- **Looks like:** two colleagues on a coffee break or friends after a morning walk; one hands the other nothing (no product exchange); the
  product on the table. Dialogue short, pronoun-free.

## 1C — Podcast set
- **Looks like:** two mics, a calm set, a 45-year-old host; the product on the desk. Shoulder-up.

## 1D — Voice-over lifestyle
- **Looks like:** quiet morning routine; a calm voice carries the bible lines; the product appears once, label to camera.

Guardrails for all: clothed day wear; ordinary faces 30–65+; no gym bodies; no needles, droppers or injecting; no clinics; nobody says "I" or "my".
