## What they have in common
1. **The form is named in the headline area:** "NAD+ INJECTION", "Now in sublingual tablets", "Now in tablet form". This is the
   bible's "lead with the choice", done by naming the form.
2. **The product is huge:**
   - a box of six green vials (S09);
   - one wet green vial (S41);
   - the giant green tablet in a box (S81);
   - the vial and the giant tablet together (S83, "the choice").
3. **Three benefit callouts or toggles** next to the product (S41 icon callouts, S83 green on/off toggles).
4. **Bold palettes:**
   - S09 bright white + red offer burst;
   - S41 warm cell-bokeh glow;
   - S81 white + deep green;
   - S83 red/black + green.
