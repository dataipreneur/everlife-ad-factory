# NAD+ art direction (2026-10-30; bible creative direction + global rules)

**Who:** adults 30–65+, all audiences: busy professionals, parents, active people who want more energy and a clearer head. An
ordinary non-famous face, clothed, calm, in real life.

**The picture is a clear head and steady energy:** bright mornings, a tidy desk with daylight, a first coffee before a full day, a
walk, a focused moment. Product-led by default. "Lead with the choice": the green bottle + one green tablet beside the green vial,
or one form per ad.

**Never:** needles, syringes, pens, injecting or "needle free"; IV drips or clinic chairs; celebrities, CEO or athlete look-alikes;
age-reversal or body imagery; the nasal spray.

**Palette:** fresh and bright by default (white, morning light, soft greys) with the product's green as the accent; warm daylight or
a dark, focused charcoal + green glow as options. **Type:** clean, modern sans or a confident serif.
