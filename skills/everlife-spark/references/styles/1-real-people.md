# Category 1 — Real People Talking

Library folders: UGC · Talking Head Avatar · AI Avatar · Conversational · Podcast · Voice Over (boards 1, 2, 4, 6).
Why it works: the highest trust for a 30–65 buyer; a real face naming the goal out loud. For Spark the face is a woman or a man (it is for both)
(the category convention) but the talk stays about the product, never about results or anyone's private life.

**Engine: Marketing Studio UGC** (`--mode ugc --product_ids @<skill>/assets/product_ids.json --duration 15 --resolution 720p --aspect_ratio 9:16 --generate-audio true`), 75 credits. Never Seedance or still-image lip-sync for these.

Universal prompt lines: "ultra-realistic creator UGC, real handheld phone shake, real skin texture, real room audio, no beauty filter; the filming phone is NEVER visible; label readable and never mirrored; fully clothed, daytime, head-and-shoulders framing; no bed or bedroom in frame; nobody says 'I' or 'my'; say the combo, if spoken, as 'P-T one-forty-one'; keep the bottom 25% free of faces."

---

## 1A — Creator to camera
- **Looks like:** one person (a woman 30–50 in a sunlit kitchen or on a terrace; a man 35–55 in a car before dinner or in a hallway) talking to their phone, product by the cheek.
- **Beats:** 0–2 a pattern-interrupt action (tears a page off a wall calendar, tosses it) → 2–6 the territory as a pronoun-free line ("Some nights aren't planned.") → 6–10 product up + ONE mechanism line ("Supports brain-based desire. Non-hormonal.") → 10–12 "Doctor prescribed. Ships free from the pharmacy."
- **Finishing:** `tiktok`. **Guardrails:** describe the product, never their results or their partner.

## 1B — Friend-to-friend
- **Looks like:** two friends 35–65 at a café or on a walk; A asks what the small bottle is; B describes the product: "Spark. Non-hormonal desire support, on demand. For men and women."
- **Guardrails:** Rx never shared; nothing about anyone's nights; the off-camera voice never testifies.
- **Finishing:** `default` (house look).

## 1C — Podcast set
- **Looks like:** two hosts at a warm podcast desk, the product on the desk.
- **Beats:** the host's hot take ("Desire starts in the brain, not just blood flow") → each host gives ONE approved benefit → product on desk.
- **Finishing:** `editorial`. **Guardrails:** no "studies show" for the product; no "ED".

## 1D — Voice-over lifestyle
- **Looks like:** a calm narrator over 3–4 candid daytime shots of one person (coffee, a walk, getting ready at a counter, a laugh with a friend), benefit chips added in finishing.
- **Finishing:** `default` (house look) or `editorial`.
