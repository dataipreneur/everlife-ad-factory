## How to use these cards
- Take **layout, format, hook mechanics, staging, palette and type** from a card. Take **every word** from `product-bible.md`
  (✅ lines; ⏳/⚠ only with the user's OK).
- **Never copy the "don't copy" items.** Most of these predate today's rules.

## What the winners have in common
1. **The tablet is the hero:** huge, close to the lens, often bigger than the bottle (S03, S11, S13). The bottle is secondary.
   Bible 2026-11-03 replaced that rule with the focal-point mix (40% tablet / 30% bottle / 30% person): use this look for the tablet-focused slots.
2. **"SPARK" huge, in the logotype:** a wide serif with the star inside the A, in rose-gold or gold.
3. **Three benefit "pills" with line icons** (brain-based / on-demand / non-hormonal), stacked or in a row.
4. **Warm, premium palettes:**
   - S03 gold-amber;
   - S04 teal + gold;
   - S08 cream + orange;
   - S13 blush + crystal;
   - S11 golden hour;
   - S10 navy + cream;
   - S14 black + gold.
5. **Elegant women lead the people ads** (S04 back of a gown, S10 profile, S11 golden-hour portrait). S14 is a couple, dressed and
   outdoors.
6. **Video:**
   - V02 is product-only luxury (red roses, velvet, gold, slow push-ins, "Save up to 51%" end).
   - **V04 and V06 are 3D-animated couples** (Pixar-like): a bored evening becomes a spark of connection; in V06 a waiter serves
     the tablet on a cushion.
   - A persistent header, benefit chips and an offer badge throughout.
