# Which skill to copy

Copy the closest existing skill, then adapt every file. The script suggests one from the bible's category label; confirm it.

| New product looks like… | Copy | Why | Watch out |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Intimate wellness / sexual health** (Lovara, Spark, Titan-rx) | `everlife-pt-141` | Tasteful, product-led flow; personas critique; no bedrooms; hook-angle question | One form per ad; Spark never ties a benefit to one ingredient; Lovara is women-only |
