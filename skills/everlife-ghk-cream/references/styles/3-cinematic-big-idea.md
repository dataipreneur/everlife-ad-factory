# Category 3 — Cinematic Big Idea

Library folders: Movie-Peg · Wild-Creative/Surreal · Retro · New Year · Billboard (boards 1, 3, 4, 5, 6).
Why it works: a gentle pattern interrupt in a feed full of identical skincare ads; warm and tasteful. (The team rejected
surreal-from-scratch videos: keep it real, object-based, 720p.)

## 3A — Object metaphor
- **Looks like:** a silk ribbon pulled gently taut; a creased beige linen sheet smoothed flat by a hand; morning light sliding across a
  blush plaster wall. The bottle arrives in the final beat, label to camera. One a bible reference line line in finishing ("Firmer-Looking Skin Starts
  With Support").
- **Guardrails:** the metaphor is about support and smoothness of objects, never a face or body transforming.

## 3B — The cleared shelf
- **Looks like:** a crowded bathroom shelf of anonymous jars and tubes (plain, unbranded) → the same frame with just the bottle; one
  a bible reference line line ("Your Daily GHK Glow"); "Upgrade Your Skin Routine".
- **Guardrails:** no competitor brands or readable labels; no "throw away your products" claims.

## 3C — Retro film
- **Looks like:** 70s film grain in warm beige and rose (never orange), a woman 40–55 laughing at a café table with a friend, the bottle
  on the table.

## 3D — Seasonal
- **Looks like:** a new-year routine, Mother's Day, a summer morning; the bottle on a dressing table. No orange autumn or green spring
  palettes: keep blush, tan and beige.
