# Category 6 — Lifestyle & Identity

Library folders: Female Glow Up · Life Cell · Sports · Elite/Celebrity Secret · Winners (boards 1, 2, 3, 5).
Why it works: the woman 30–55 (core demo: the 40-year-old mom) sees herself; a calm, capable morning; beauty and fitness without the
gym-body clichés. Always candid, sun-lit, pinks/tans/beige, never orange or green; never a before/after.

## 6A — One step, busy morning
- **Looks like:** a mom 38–45 between the school run and work: keys in one hand, one pump of the cream at the hallway mirror shelf,
  out the door laughing. "Your Daily GHK Glow."
- **Guardrails:** shoulder-up / waist-up; the mirror shows no "reveal"; the kids, if any, are background and never the subject.
- **Finishing:** `tiktok` or `editorial`.

## 6B — After the run
- **Looks like:** a woman 35–50 back from a morning run or yoga class in everyday activewear (blush, tan, white; never green), lean and
  naturally fit, at her kitchen counter with water and the bottle. "Peptide-Powered Skin Support."
- **Guardrails:** never muscular, never posed, no body focus, no gym equipment, no sweat close-ups; identity, not transformation.

## 6C — Her glow
- **Looks like:** a woman 40–55 in warm (not orange) morning light at a vanity, laughing mid-moment; the bottle on the counter.
  "Glow Smarter With GHK Peptide Cream" (FB/GL/TK only).
- **Guardrails:** shoulder-up; no beauty-filter gloss; no skin close-up.
