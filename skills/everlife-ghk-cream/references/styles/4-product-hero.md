# Category 4 — Product as Hero  (highest fit)

Library folders: Product-as-Hero · Product Highlight · Pill-Product Focused · Minimalist · Polaroid (boards 3, 4, 5).
Why it works: premium by construction; the default for GHK cream statics (Mitansh's premium-DTC direction:
`core/references/static-refs/13_Premium_DTC/`). The pump bottle is the hero (there is no tablet), large and legible.

## 4A — Vanity still life
- **Looks like:** the bottle upright on travertine or a beige linen runner, blush silk folds, soft morning daylight, one real shadow.
  Serif Tier A line.
- Static: batch F12. Video: Seedance slow push-in, 480p is enough.

## 4B — Pump macro
- **Looks like:** a macro of the champagne-gold pump head as a finger presses it once, the navy label sharp below. The cream stays out
  of frame (not on file). Never a second bottle.
- Static: batch F2/F11. Video: Seedance macro orbit.

## 4C — Minimal bold
- **Looks like:** one bottle, one colour field (blush, sand, nude, warm white), one huge headline, up to three short lines.

## 4D — Ad in a frame
- **Looks like:** the ad as a framed print in a hallway or a postcard on a dressing table.

## 4E — Morning-routine ASMR
- **Looks like:** hands set the bottle down on a bathroom counter among plain everyday objects (a watch, a hair clip, a cup of tea); a
  tap runs; a towel (white or beige, never green) folds. Shoulder-up if a face appears; the mirror shows no face "reveal".
- **Guardrails:** no other skincare brands; no droppers, jars or serums beside it.
