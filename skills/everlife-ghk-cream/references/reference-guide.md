# Which reference to use when (GHK Peptide Cream)

Order (`core/references/REFERENCE-PRIORITY.md`): **1** our own GHK cream winners (none on file 2026-11-02: no rows in the Meta
all-time export, nothing in `~/Everlife-Creative/reference-ads/`; ask Emily) → **2** Meta Ad Library refs in `core/references/meta-refs/`
→ **3** the shared Pinterest library `core/references/static-refs/` (folders by framework; overview `OVERVIEW.jpg`). It teaches
**layout, type scale, product staging and mood**, never words or scenes. Pick ONE pin per slot, look at it, and borrow only its structure.

Skincare reference ads are full of things this product can't do: before/after faces, "erase wrinkles", "clinically proven", "years
younger", dermatologist claims, orange/green "clean beauty" palettes, droppers and serum jars. Layout only.

| Angle (bible) | Best frameworks | In the folder, pick the pin that is… | Avoid in that folder |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Firmness** — "Firmer-Looking Skin Starts With Support", "Support Firmer Skin" | F2 Bold Claim · F12 Brand Mood · F13 Portrait + line | 02: big-type, one-idea pins over calm space, product lower third; 12: beige/blush still lifes; 13 Premium DTC tiles | any face close-up comparison, "lifted" arrows |
| **The peptide step** — "Peptide-Powered Skin Support", "Your Daily GHK Glow" | F4 Features & Benefits · F9 Reasons Why (advertorial on white) · F10 Sticky Note | 04: three-icon-row pins with ONE product and lots of air; 09: numbered "3 reasons"; 10: a note on a mirror or planner | 04's neon/3D pins; molecule diagrams |
| **Glow** — "Glow Smarter With GHK Peptide Cream" (FB/GL/TK), "Encourages a Brighter, More Even-Looking Tone" | F12 · F11 Visual Metaphor · F13 | 12: warm morning-light vanity still lifes; 11: single-object metaphor pins | glossy "glass skin" close-ups, orange golden-hour grades |
| **Trust / offer** (doctor prescribed, pharmacy, 100,000+, Save up to 75%) | F6 Offer · F2 · F12 | 06 percent-off (never $); 12 pharmacy-feel still lifes | 07 until reviews exist |

Rules of thumb:
- One promise per ad and one plain line that explains it.
- If a pin's mood is "before/after" or "dermatology clinic", take only its type scale and leave the scene.
- Never reuse the same pin for two slots in one batch; never the same framework twice unless the batch is bigger than 8.
- Colours: pinks, tans and beige; no oranges or greens (`image-batch.md` §2b).
