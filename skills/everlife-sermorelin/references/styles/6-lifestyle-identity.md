# Category 6 — Lifestyle & Identity

Library folders: Hero Fitness Guy · Sports · Fighter UFC · Dad Bod · Female Glow Up · Life Cell (boards 2, 3, 5).
What the library shows: gym heroes, runners, tennis, UFC cages, "kiss your dad-bod goodbye", glow-up portraits. The past plain-Sermorelin ads lean hard on this (shirtless heroes, "Same man" splits, abs): today's rules ban almost all of it.
Why it still works: the bible frames Sermorelin around **muscle, fitness and performance** and healthy aging for both audiences. Identity ("still in the game at 55", "Thrive After 35") sells without a body claim.

**Hard NOs here:** body composition, fat, "dad bod", "new 40" (⚠ Jeremy), "physique" transformations, shirtless or bodybuilder heroes, exposed abs, gym-mirror body shots, before/after, age reversal, bedroom. Performance comes from *activity and attitude*, never from showing a changed body.
**Engine:** Marketing Studio UGC for people (75 cr), Seedance for multi-shot montages (45 cr), GPT Image 2.5 statics.

## 6A — Prime-time training (men)
- **Looks like:** a rugged 42–50 "fit silver fox" with a hardcore fitness-model build, fully clothed in a fitted training tee, early-morning sun: a heavy farmer's carry, a sled push, a rowing sprint, a boxing bag. Real sweat, hard-training intensity; no flexing.
- **Beats:** 0–2 a sound-led hook (a kettlebell set down hard, breath) → 2–6 the effort → 6–10 the tablet in his palm at the gym bag, bottle beside it → 10–12 he nods and goes again.
- **Copy:** men Tier A (*Built for Men Who Still Want More*); benefit *Supports Strength + Vitality*.
- **Finishing:** `house`.
