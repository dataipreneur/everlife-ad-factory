# Category 4 — Product as Hero  (top fit for Sermorelin)

Library folders: Product Highlight · Pill-Product Focused · Minimalist · Product-as-Hero · Polaroid · Billboard (boards 1, 3, 4, 5).
Why it works: the bible's first composition rule is "the pill/tablet is the hero"; the past plain-Sermorelin ads that hold up best are the tablet held to the lens and the name run huge.

**Statics:** GPT Image 2.5 via Creatify with bottle + tablet + type specimen attached (~1 cr). **Video:** Seedance 2.0 or Boreal-style for a single locked shot. Tablet prompt line, always: "use the attached tablet reference exactly; do not invent, alter, or substitute a different pill." Plus: "no vial or second container."

---

## 4A — Premium still life
- **Men:** the tablet large in the foreground on warm stone or a chalked barbell collar, bottle behind, sun-lit, texture and depth. **Women:** the tablet on blush linen or travertine with soft morning light (the orange tablet is the only orange in frame).
- **Video:** slow push-in, the tablet settling in slow motion, light sweeping across.
- **Finishing:** `finish_batch.py` (F2 / F12).

## 4B — Tablet in hand
- **Looks like:** an extreme close-up of a 45+ hand (real skin) holding ONE tablet up to warm window light; a gym or kitchen softly out of focus. (The past ads' strongest layout; keep the hand mature and the background calm.)
- **Guardrails:** exactly one tablet; no lips/mouth macro (past "tablet on the tongue" crops read as intimate); no swallowing several.

## 4C — Minimal bold name
- **Looks like:** dark textured background, single key light on the tablet + bottle, "SERMORELIN" or one short Tier A headline set huge (men: *Double Strength Sermorelin* only with the confirmed bottle), a 3-benefit strip.

## 4D — Ad in a frame
- **Looks like:** a hand holding a phone or polaroid showing the ad, a gym-locker poster, a billboard at dawn. Generate the scene with a blank frame; finishing places the ad in it (`finish_image.py --layout frame`).
- **Guardrails:** no fake handles, like counts or brand names.

## 4E — Morning routine ASMR
- **Looks like:** top-down counter at sunrise: gym bag zip, water glass, the tablet placed under the tongue (sublingual), keys, out the door.
- **Guardrails:** plain props with no text; exactly ONE tablet; say "easy sublingual delivery" only.
- **Finishing:** `editorial`.
