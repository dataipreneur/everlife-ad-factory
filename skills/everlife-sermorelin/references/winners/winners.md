## How to use these cards (read before writing a batch)
- Take **layout, format, hook mechanics, mood and product staging** from a card. Take **every word** from the bible
  (Tier A) or as an approved Tier B line. The image model never sees these ads, only you do.
- **Never copy the "Don't copy" items.** Every one of these winners breaks at least one of today's rules (shirtless
  bodies, before/after, fat claims, "HGH", injections, $ prices, old proof, non-bible disclaimers).
- Small samples (3–5 purchases) can be luck; weight **M5 BASE130-01** (43 purchases, $10.5k spend) and **M4 VIDEO82B** (8).

## What our winners have in common
1. **"Double Strength" / "2X" / "Upgrade your Sermorelin" is the hook** in 4 of 5 men's winners (bible-approved men's
   headline "Double Strength Sermorelin"; needs the confirmed 1000 mcg bottle in frame).
2. **"SERMORELIN" set huge**, often in orange, at the top.
3. **The round orange "S" tablet** in every men's winner, usually 2–3 tablets in the foreground next to the bottle. (The
   older women's winner shows the oblong tablet stamped "SERMORELIN".)
4. **Dark gym mood + orange accents** for men.
5. **A short benefit list (3–4 lines with icons)** and a proof footer.
6. **None** of them use a calm, sun-lit, clothed lifestyle look: that's untested for this product, not proven to fail.
