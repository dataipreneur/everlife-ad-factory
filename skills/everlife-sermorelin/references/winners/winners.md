# Sermorelin past ads: what to keep, what not to copy

**No CPA-ranked winners on file yet.** The Sermorelin GHK skill's winners are a different product: never use them here.
When the creative team's "Winning Ads Reference" page covers plain Sermorelin, add CPA-ranked cards here in the same
format as the GHK skill's `winners/winners.md` (Format · Works · Don't copy · Rebuild with), with small preview images.

Source of the notes below: the user's downloads "Sermorelin M" (≈230 files) and "Sermorelin F" (≈70 files) from Drive,
Oct 16 2026: past plain-Sermorelin ads (men "Sermorelin 2X / Double Strength", women "Get Toned and Lean" era), looked at
on 2026-10-28 as contact sheets. Unranked: these show what was made, not what won. Images are not copied into the repo.

## What the past ads show about the product
- **Bottle:** amber jar, orange label, vertical EVERLIFE wordmark, "RX" box. Labels not legible at preview size: read the
  real label off the asset-library render.
- **Tablet:** two shapes appear: a **round orange tablet with an embossed "S"** (nearly all men's ads, many women's) and an
  **oblong orange tablet stamped "SERMORELIN"** (several women's ads). Confirm which is current (`assets/MISSING_RENDERS.md`).
- **Vial:** almost every ad stands an orange-capped injection vial next to the bottle. Out of scope for this skill.

## Patterns worth keeping (layout and staging only; words always from the bible)
1. **Tablet held to the lens** by a real hand, big and sharp, person or scene soft behind (men "Take Sermorelin Up a
   Level" layout; women "power of growth hormone" layout). Cleanest, most product-led format in the set.
2. **Product name run huge** ("SERMORELIN" in heavy condensed or repeated-word typography with the tablet over it).
3. **Three benefit icons** in a row under the product.
4. **Warm golden-hour still life** (tablet + bottle on a ledge at sunset, men's "Double Strength" layout).
5. **Proof footer** (Trustpilot, Doctor Prescribed, Ships Free From Pharmacy): now added by finishing.

## Don't copy (seen in the past ads; all break today's rules)
- Shirtless bodybuilders, exposed abs, sports-bra ab shots; "Same man, double strength" and "From Flab to Fab" body
  splits; any before/after.
- "Breaking News"; "Get Ripped and Lean"; "Fat burning", "Promote Fat Loss", "Targets Visceral Fat", "Burn"; "Sculpt your
  body"; "Lost the weight with GLP-1s? … Tighten excess skin"; "Sermorelin is a peptide just like the GLP-1s".
- "Skip the needles, go sublingual"; "Sublingual tablets or injections"; "Choose injections or sublingual tablets";
  any vial.
- "Starting at $95", "Best price"; "Over 750,000 sublingual tablets sold"; "Featured in NIH / Mayo Clinic" logos.
- "Sermorelin GH Peptide" / "GH"; "Increases human growth hormone"; "The power of growth hormone in a tablet" (⚠).
- Fake bylined articles ("By Brian L."); lips/tongue macro crops; fire, beast-eyes and lightning backdrops.
- Typos in the source ads ("SEMORELIN").
