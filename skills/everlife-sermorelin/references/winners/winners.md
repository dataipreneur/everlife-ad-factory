# Sermorelin winners: what works, what not to copy

Source: the creative team's "Winning Ads Reference" page (claude.ai artifact 5gDbB7ibXwcNoWnKqcU9Sc), product cards
"Sermorelin — Male" (report week **Oct 9–15, 2026**) and "Sermorelin — Female" (stale: no eligible winner Oct 9–15, shows
the **Oct 2–8** winner). CPA = spend ÷ purchases. Files matched by code to the user's Drive downloads "Sermorelin M" /
"Sermorelin F" (Oct 16 2026) and looked at on 2026-10-28. Previews here are small (`<code>.jpg`); full files are on Drive
(links on the page). **Refresh when the page updates.** The Sermorelin GHK winners are a different product: never use them here.

That page's catalog files three products under the Sermorelin card: **Sermorelin 1000 mcg** sublingual tablet
("double-strength"), **Sermorelin 500 mcg** sublingual tablet, and the **Sermorelin 3 mg injection** (out of scope for
creative). The men's winners are mostly 1000 mcg "Double Strength" ads.

## How to use these cards (read before writing a batch)
- Take **layout, format, hook mechanics, mood and product staging** from a card. Take **every word** from the bible
  (Tier A) or as an approved Tier B line. The image model never sees these ads, only you do.
- **Never copy the "Don't copy" items.** Every one of these winners breaks at least one of today's rules (shirtless
  bodies, before/after, fat claims, "HGH", injections, $ prices, old proof, non-bible disclaimers).
- Small samples (3–5 purchases) can be luck; weight **M5 BASE130-01** (43 purchases, $10.5k spend) and **M4 VIDEO82B** (8).

## What our winners have in common
1. **"Double Strength" / "2X" / "Upgrade your Sermorelin" is the hook** in 4 of 5 men's winners (bible-approved men's
   headline "Double Strength Sermorelin"; needs the confirmed 1000 mcg bottle in frame).
2. **"SERMORELIN" set huge**, often in orange, at the top.
3. **The round orange "S" tablet** in every men's winner, usually 2–3 tablets in the foreground next to the bottle. (The
   older women's winner shows the oblong tablet stamped "SERMORELIN".)
4. **Dark gym mood + orange accents** for men.
5. **A short benefit list (3–4 lines with icons)** and a proof footer.
6. **None** of them use a calm, sun-lit, clothed lifestyle look: that's untested for this product, not proven to fail.

### M2 · INT91 · static · ~$208 CPA (4, $832) · `M2_INT91.jpg`
- **Format:** bold claim, F2. Black-and-white gritty poster, "DOUBLE STRENGTH / SERMORELIN" huge at the top (name in
  orange italic), a man curling a dumbbell whose plate is embossed with an "S", a right-hand column of three stat blocks
  ("2X", "50%", "40%"), a stacked three-line power headline bottom-left.
- **Works:** the "S" tablet echoed as a dumbbell plate (visual metaphor, F11); mono photo + one orange accent; big stat
  numbers; three-line stacked headline.
- **Don't copy:** shirtless bodybuilder; "Look Leaner" (body composition), "Age Better"; "50% more potent INJECTIONS";
  "Up to 40% off" (bible offer is **"Save up to 65%"**); no product, no tablet, no proof in frame.
- **Rebuild with:** a clothed fit man 50+ in the same mono gritty gym; the dumbbell-plate "S" metaphor; headline
  "Double Strength Sermorelin" (Tier A); the real tablet + bottle in the foreground; stat column → "Save up to 65%" +
  two ✅ benefits.

### M3 · ID323819-3.01 (+ 1.01) · static · ~$214 CPA (5, $1,069) · `M3_ID323819-3.01.jpg`, `M3b_ID323819-1.01.jpg`
Page note: the **1.01 cut is "crushing it" ($274 CPA / 26 sales in 5 days)**: both iterations linked. 1.01 = orange name
box + warm orange light; 3.01 = grey name box + cooler light. Same copy.
- **Format:** features & benefits, F4. "SERMORELIN" in a solid box top-left, "Growth Hormone Peptide" under it, four icon
  benefit lines, a man on the right, bottle + vial + one big "S" tablet bottom-left, "SAVE 70% VS HGH" badge.
- **Works:** name-in-a-box header; four icon lines, big and readable; one oversized tablet cut off the frame edge;
  warm orange version beat the grey one.
- **Don't copy:** shirtless tattooed bodybuilder; "Increases Human Growth Hormone" ("HGH" icon), "Support Fat Loss",
  "Lean Muscle Definition" (⚠); "SAVE 70% VS HGH" (⚠ Jeremy); the vial; "Over 750,000 sublingual tablets sold"; "free
  rush shipping"; a disclaimer that isn't the bible's.
- **Rebuild with:** clothed fit man 50+; three ✅ benefits; "Save up to 65%" badge; bible footer; warm orange-on-dark
  (the 1.01 look) for men.

### M4 · sermorelin-m-video#82b-1.01 · video, 19 s, 9:16 + 1:1 · ~$223 CPA (8, $1,785) · `M4_VIDEO82B.jpg` (contact sheet)
- **Format:** surreal creature battle: a sea dragon and a shark fight over the bottle in a stormy ocean, then underwater,
  ending on a dragon-man. Fixed header "UPGRADE YOUR SERMORELIN / NOW DOUBLE STRENGTH", word-by-word captions, a fixed
  benefit + proof + disclaimer block over the bottom third. VO: "The strongest Sermorelin on the market just got better.
  Everlife offers double strength 1000 microgram tablets and 3 milligram injections. Maximum potency. Same price. Step up
  the game today. Experience Sermorelin now. Shipped free from the pharmacy."
- **Works:** a pattern-interrupt creature image from frame 1; the "upgrade / double strength" news angle; a fixed header
  so the offer reads with sound off; bottle in nearly every frame.
- **Don't copy:** "the strongest … on the market" (unsupported superiority), "Maximum potency", injections, the vial;
  "Trusted by 70,000+" (now **100,000+**); "Lean Muscle Definition" (⚠); 19 s with the CTA buried; dragon/shark gore
  (the creative lead rejected surreal-from-scratch: see lessons). A Restora-Z-style "surreal" rebuild needs care.
- **Rebuild with:** keep the "Now Double Strength" news header and sound-off design (Tier A "Double Strength
  Sermorelin"); a grounded pattern interrupt instead of creatures (3A movie-peg, 5B versus-a-problem); VO ≤ 35 words,
  15 s, end card with a bible CTA.

### M5 · BASE130-01 · static · ~$244 CPA (**43 purchases, $10,474**) · `M5_BASE130-01.jpg`
The highest-volume Sermorelin ad by far.
- **Format:** body before/after: "SERMORELIN / DOUBLE STRENGTH." huge in the centre, "Same man, double strength", the
  same man soft and shirtless on the left vs ripped and glowing on the right, three icon lines, bottle + vial + three "S"
  tablets, "Starting at $95" badge, Trustpilot / Doctor Prescribed / Ships Free footer, bible disclaimer.
- **Works:** the huge two-line name + "DOUBLE STRENGTH." lockup; orange fire-glow palette on black; tablets piled in front
  of the bottle; the proof footer.
- **Don't copy (all banned):** the body before/after and "Same man" split; shirtless bodies; "This isn't genetics";
  "Unlock your body potential", "Support the stronger version of you"; "$95"; the vial.
- **Rebuild with:** the name lockup and orange-on-black palette, ONE clothed fit man 50+ (no second state), tablets in
  front of the bottle, ✅ benefits, "Save up to 65%". The lift came from the transformation promise, which can't be used:
  expect a rebuild to perform differently and test it.

## Women (1, stale)

### F1 · ID273400-3.04 · static · ~$494 CPA (3, $1,482) · Oct 2–8 · `F1_ID273400-3.04.jpg`
- **Format:** product hero / minimal bold name, 4C. One big **oblong** orange tablet stamped "SERMORELIN" over
  "SERMORELIN" repeated five times in grey on a dark teal textured background, "SUBLINGUAL GROWTH HORMONE PEPTIDE" kicker,
  three check lines.
- **Works:** tablet as the single hero, huge; repeated-name typography; no person needed.
- **Don't copy:** "Skip the needles, go sublingual" (no needles, even negated); "Fat-burning"; "Muscle-boosting";
  teal-green background (women: no greens); no proof footer, no disclaimer; the oblong tablet unless it's confirmed current.
- **Rebuild with:** blush/tan/beige textured background, the confirmed tablet render huge, repeated "SERMORELIN" type,
  "Easy sublingual delivery" + two ✅ benefits, bible footer. Expensive CPA on 3 purchases: weak signal.
