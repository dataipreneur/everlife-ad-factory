## Product facts for this skill
- **Product name on creative:** "Sermorelin". Never "Sermorelin GHK", never "GH" ("growth hormone" spelled out), never "HGH"
  except inside the ⚠ offer line below.
- **What it is:** a doctor-prescribed, compounded growth hormone–releasing peptide. Peptides *support the body's own
  production*: that is the limit. Mechanism first ("supports natural growth hormone activity"), then the downstream benefit.
- **Form this skill advertises: the sublingual tablet only.** The bible says Sermorelin is "available as sublingual
  tablets or injections", but creative never mentions or shows needles, injections, vials or syringes (global rule), so
  injections are out of scope. Say "easy sublingual delivery". Never "all natural".
- **The tablet is the hero.** Which tablet is current is not confirmed yet (see `assets/MISSING_RENDERS.md`): past ads
  show both a round orange tablet with an embossed "S" and an oblong orange tablet stamped "SERMORELIN".
- **Strength:** the men's page approves "Double Strength Sermorelin" and "Sublingual tablets with 2x the strength". Which
  bottle is the double-strength one is not confirmed: until the asset-library render says so, copy may use the bible's
  "Double Strength" headline only if the bottle shown is confirmed double strength (ask the user).
- **Audiences (pick one per session):** **Men 35–65+** (lean muscle, energy, strength, healthy aging; frame around muscle,
  fitness and performance, never the bedroom; fit, mature talent, not bodybuilders) · **Women 35–65+** (lean muscle,
  energy, graceful aging; palette pinks, tans, beige, **no oranges or greens** in the scene).
- **Orange product vs women's palette:** the bottle label and tablet are orange. The product stays as it is (never
  recolour it); keep the scene, type and accents in pink/tan/beige so the product is the only orange. ⚠ Open question for
  the creative lead.
- **Offer:** "Save up to 65%" (percent-off, usable). No $ prices by default (creative standard: percent-off unless a price
  point is being tested); "Starting at $95/month" only when a brief asks for a price test.
- **Proof:** "Trustpilot rated 4.9 Excellent" · "Over 100,000 happy customers" · "24/7 access to doctor support".
- **Disclaimer (verbatim, both audiences):** `AI-generated depictions and simulated portrayals used for illustrative advertising purposes. Not actual patients or medical providers. Disclaimer: Individual results may vary. Doctor-prescribed compounded prescription.`
- **Landing page:** https://everlifemd.com/sermorelin/v1L_v2f (last verified Aug. 28, 2026). Facts only; offers and
  claims come from this page, never from the landing page.
