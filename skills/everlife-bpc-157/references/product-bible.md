## The angle (agreed with Mitansh, 2026-10-29: "Mix: a different angle per ad"; 4th angle added the same day)
Rotate four angles across every batch; each ad gets one:
1. **Recovery is the bottleneck.** Effort is covered; recovery isn't. Foam rollers, ice baths, protein and stretching are not a
   recovery plan. Direct, a little blunt. ("Your Foam Roller Isn't Enough", "Don't Let Recovery Be the Bottleneck", "Train Hard. Recover Smarter.")
2. **The comeback.** Getting back to the thing you love after a strain or a setback. Warmer, more story, people-led.
   ("Built to Bounce Back", "The Comeback Starts Here", "Less Setback. More Comeback.", "Recovery Starts Before You're Sidelined")
3. **More than muscle, inside out.** Recovery is body-wide: joints, tendons, connective tissue and gut lining. Calm, premium, product-led.
   ("Gut Support Meets Recovery", "Recovery Starts Deeper", "For More Than Muscle", "Support the System That Supports You")
4. **Problem → solution.** Name the gap first, then the support: training is covered, recovery lags; the rest day isn't a plan; the
   stack is missing something. The problem is always a *recovery* gap, never an injury, pain or a body part (global gates), and never
   a pronoun question. Layout: a split or a two-step ("Problem: … / Solution: …", old way → new way, ✗ list → ✓ line).
   ("Recovery Starts Before You're Sidelined", "Stop Treating Recovery Like an Afterthought", "Recovery Needs More Than Rest Days",
   "Your Recovery Stack Is Missing Something", "There's More to Recovery Than Sleep", "Don't Let Recovery Lag Behind" (women))
Men's register: plain, earned, no hype, and the picture is HARDCORE (bodybuilders, tattoos, loaded bars; `mens-direction.md`).
Women's register: strong and soft at once; "glow" only as mood, never as a skin promise.
