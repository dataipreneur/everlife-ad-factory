## What to borrow (layout, staging, type; never the words)
- **Vial as hero, big, label to camera, tilted 10–20°** off vertical with the cap toward a corner (fridays, kin, PauseRX, Protide).
  Two or three vials stepped in depth read as "a system" (Protide, kin V27): for us ONE vial, always.
- **Monochrome harmony:** the label colour becomes the field (fridays green-on-green, RM red-on-red, PauseRX slate-on-slate,
  fridays Sermorelin teal). Our vial is slate blue: slate / charcoal / chalk fields, or black + slate.
- **Hand holding the vial between thumb and finger**, nothing else in frame (kin V18/V19, Aurelius V09, NAD+ V14/V33, GLP-1 V41/V42): a clean,
  trusted "pharmacy" cue. Male hand for men's ads: chalk, veins, a wrap.
- **One huge word or product name vertical / cropped** (PauseRX "GLUTATHIONE", "NAD+"): good with the heavy condensed type in §2b.
- **Benefit pills** as rounded tags beside the vial (fridays V30/S5): max 3, our quick-hit lines only.
- **Water/splash and "cellular" glow backdrops** (Protide, SS31 V32, green splash V38, PeterMD V39): fine for the quiet variant; never medical anatomy.

## Compliance traps in these references (never copy)
"Heals / healing / repair / treat pain / regenerate / anti-inflammatory / fat loss / muscle growth" claims · "Injections designed to…"
and any syringe · "Surgical alternative" · "research use only" / "99% purity" · $ prices and "$2/day" · "clinically", "science-backed"
· competitor brand names · "Wolverine" · needle-free wording. Our copy = the seven quick-hit lines, our proof row, "Take up to 40% Off".
