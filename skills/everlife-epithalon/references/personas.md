# Persona critique — the target buyer reads the ad before Mitansh does

Run this on EVERY concept (image or video) after writing it and BEFORE the approval question. It is free: no tools, no credits.
Write the critique in the persona's voice, first person, 3–5 lines, timestamped for video or top-to-bottom for a static.
Then list the fixes you made because of it. Show the critique under the concept so Mitansh sees what Glen and Maria saw.
(The personas speak in first person here only; ad copy never does.)

## Persona 1 — "Gardening Glen", 61
Retired teacher, walks every morning, grandchildren on weekends, reads the label before he buys anything. Distrusts "anti-aging"
miracles and anything that looks like a lab-coat infomercial.
What Glen punishes: "reverse", "younger", "turn back the clock"; jargon with no plain line ("oxidative stress" needs the scene or a
plain line beside it); tiny or thin type; needles; anything that promises a result or a timeline.

## Persona 2 — "Maria", 52
Runs a small business, does yoga twice a week, reads about longevity, shops carefully online.
What Maria punishes: before/after or mirror-face images; DNA-helix "science" graphics; sleep promises (she already knows the
product page says it isn't a sleep product); anything that feels like a cream ad or a diet product; stock-photo couples.

## The rubric (score each 1–5; anything under 4 gets fixed before approval)
1. **Clear in 2 seconds:** what it is (one vial), who it's for, the one promise ("Supports Healthy Aging").
2. **Plain English:** every claim word is explained by the scene or a bible line; no jargon stack.
3. **Not scammy:** real product, real pharmacy proof, no cure or reversal words, no timing numbers, no needles.
4. **Looks like me:** a real adult 40–65 in a calm, sun-lit daytime moment, not a model or a physique.
5. **Worth a click:** the end card answers "what do I do now" (a ✅ CTA; no offer line until the bible has one).

Write the critique honestly. If Glen or Maria wouldn't click, say so and fix the concept before showing it.
