# Category 6 — Lifestyle & Identity (Epithalon)

Why it works: the 40–65 buyer sees themselves engaged, capable and calm. Healthy aging, never reversal (`../direction.md`); never
a needle; never a bed, night or wake-up; no mirrors.

## 6A — The garden
- **Looks like:** a man or woman 50–65 in a sun-lit vegetable garden, hands in the soil, unhurried; the vial on the potting bench.
- **Finishing:** `default` or `editorial`.

## 6B — Family afternoon
- **Looks like:** a grandparent 55–65 on the patio with grandchildren, a ball, lemonade; the vial on the table. "Promotes Healthy Aging."

## 6C — Market and kitchen
- **Looks like:** a man or woman 45–60 choosing produce at a farmers' market, then the kitchen table in daylight; the vial beside the bowl.

## 6D — Morning walk / hike
- **Looks like:** a couple of friends (not a stock couple) 50–65 on a trail in morning sun; walking jackets; the vial on the
  kitchen counter after.
- **Guardrails (all):** never a physique shot; no before/after; no younger-vs-older; no weight or food-plate imagery; daytime only.
