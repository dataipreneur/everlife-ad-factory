# Category 3 — Cinematic Big Idea

Library folders: Movie-Peg · Wild-Creative/Surreal · Cause & Effect · Retro · New Year (boards 1, 2, 3, 5, 6).

## 3A — Object metaphor
- **Looks like:** an old oak in morning sun, a well-kept garden tool, a sourdough starter fed again, a vintage bike oiled and ridden:
  one object, slow, real light. The vial lands last on real material. Line from the ✅ list ("Supports Healthy Aging").
- **Never:** an hourglass or clock running backwards, a face "de-aging", anything that says reversal.

## 3B — Comedic contrast
- **Looks like:** a counter crowded with ten supplement jars (unbranded), then calm: one vial on linen. Line from the ✅ list.
  Never a brand, never a body or face gag, never a before/after.

## 3C — Animated cell-defense
- **Looks like:** a clean line animation of a calm cell with a soft protective glow (no anatomy, no DNA, no medical labels), landing
  on the real vial. "Supports Oxidative Stress Defense".
- **Guardrails:** no "repairs / reverses / activates" captions; the animation ends on the real product within 10 s.

## 3D — Retro film · 3E — Seasonal
- **Looks like:** 16 mm grain on a family garden afternoon; a spring garden or an autumn market. Daytime only. Same copy rules.
