# Category 4 — Product as Hero

Library folders: Product-as-Hero · Product Highlight · Pill-Product Focused · Minimalist · Polaroid (boards 3, 4, 5).
Why it works: the vial, with its teal label, is a distinctive object; the Premium DTC codes (`13_Premium_DTC`) suit it.

## 4A — Still life
- **Looks like:** the vial on warm stone in late-morning sun, on linen beside a cup of tea, or on walnut for the quiet variant; hard
  side light, one long shadow. Sentence-case ✅ line. Static: batch F12 / F14. Video: Seedance slow push-in, 480p.
- **Guardrails:** the vial is a still object; no syringe, pen, dropper or droplets; no capsules; never on a bedside table.

## 4B — Vial macro
- **Looks like:** a macro of the label and cap in daylight (the label reads EPITALON, untouched).
- Static: batch F2 / F11. Video: Seedance macro orbit.

## 4C — Minimal bold
- **Looks like:** one vial, one colour field (teal on teal, warm sand, stone, chalk; the label's teal as the accent), "Epithalon"
  huge, one ✅ line.

## 4D — Ad in a frame
- **Looks like:** the ad as a poster in a sunny pharmacy window or a card on a kitchen corkboard.

## 4E — Pharmacy bag
- **Looks like:** a pharmacy bag opened on a sunny counter, the vial set down beside it, label to camera. Placed, never used.
