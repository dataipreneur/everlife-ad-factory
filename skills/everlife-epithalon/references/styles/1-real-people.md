# Category 1 — Real People Talking (Marketing Studio UGC, ~75 cr / 15 s)

Library folders: UGC · Talking Head Avatar · Conversational · Podcast · Voice Over (boards 2, 4, 6).
Why it works: healthy aging is a conversation adults 40–65 already have at the kitchen table, in the garden and at the pharmacy.
Needs the product registered in Marketing Studio first (Priestley's OK; preflight prints the command).

## 1A — Creator to camera (daytime)
- **Looks like:** a man or woman 45–65 at a sunny kitchen table or on a garden bench, phone propped, talking plainly. The vial sits
  in frame, label to camera; never used. One ✅ line, e.g. "Promotes Healthy Aging."
- **Lines:** ≤ 35 words, one angle, one quick-hit line, pronoun-free, no "I" (the creator talks about healthy aging, not about themselves).

## 1B — Two friends, one walk
- **Looks like:** two friends 50–65 finishing a morning walk, coffee on a bench; the vial on the bench (no product exchange).
  Dialogue short, pronoun-free.

## 1C — Podcast set
- **Looks like:** two mics, a calm daylight set, a host 50–60; the vial on the desk. Shoulder-up.

## 1D — Voice-over lifestyle
- **Looks like:** a calm daytime routine (garden, market, kitchen); a calm voice carries the bible lines; the vial appears once,
  label to camera. Never a bedtime or wake-up routine.

Guardrails for all: daytime only; no beds, night or waking up; no mirrors or skin close-ups; no needles, droppers or injecting; no
clinics; nobody says "I" or "my"; no "younger" or "reverse".
