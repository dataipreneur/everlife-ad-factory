# Epithalon art direction (2026-11-02; bible: "All audiences", "No sleep or overnight messaging — that territory is DSIP's")

**Who:** adults 40–65, men and women in balance, focused on cellular aging and healthy aging. Healthy, active, mature, engaged:
real and ordinary, non-famous faces ("must not resemble any celebrity"); a different person in every ad; 45+ in video (Hook Engine).
Not athletes, not bodybuilders; never shirtless or flexing.

**The picture is calm, capable daytime life, never age reversal:** a morning walk in sun, gardening, cooking with family, a hike
with a grown child, playing with grandchildren, reading at a sunny kitchen table, a pottery or yoga class, a desk by an open
window, a pharmacy-counter pickup (pharmacy framing = trust cue for 45+). Warm, sun-lit, candid (bible imagery rule). Shoulder-up or
full-body in natural movement.

**Palette:** the vial's **teal** label as the accent, against warm sand, linen, stone, chalk white, soft sky blue, walnut or
charcoal; or warm daylight. No palette limit for this product (the pinks/tans/beige rule is only for GHK-Cu Cream and Glow Stack).
Texture and depth behind the product (stone, linen, wood, plaster, soft daylight), never flat colour.

**Type:** calm, confident: a modern serif or a clean humanist sans; legible for 50+ at story size; no shadows or outlines on type.
Nothing that looks like a supplement shop, an anti-aging cream ad or a lab report.

**Never (beats any brief):**
- **Sleep or overnight anything** (product Hard NO): beds, bedrooms, pillows, pyjamas, night, moon, waking up, alarm clocks, yawns,
  bedside tables, "restful nights".
- **Needles, syringes, pens, droppers or injecting** (and the words "needle-free"): the vial is a still object.
- Before/after, younger-vs-older self, side-by-side faces or bodies, mirrors used to judge the face or body; "anti-aging",
  "reverse aging", "turn back the clock", "younger", "age-defying".
- DNA helixes, telomeres, lab-result screens or cell diagrams presented as a medical result; hospitals, IV drips, clinics.
- Scales, tape measures or any body-composition cue; disease words; "boost", "repair", "reverse", "builds".
- Another product's vial, bottle or pill (MOTS-c, BPC-157, the stacks, the GHK-Cu/Epithalon stack).
