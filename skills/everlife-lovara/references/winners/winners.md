## How to use these cards
- Take **layout, format, mood, product staging and type**. Take **every word** from `product-bible.md` (✅ lines; ⏳/⚠ only with the
  user's OK).
- **Never copy the "don't copy" items.** Most of these break today's rules:
  - lingerie, beds and bedrooms;
  - one benefit per ingredient (⚠ until Justin confirms the ingredients);
  - "boosting";
  - old proof numbers (4.8, 50,000, 70,000; today: **Trustpilot rated 4.9 Excellent · Over 100,000 happy customers**).

## What they have in common
1. **"LOVARA" huge:** a condensed terracotta wordmark (S1, S2) or a wide rose-gold serif (S3).
2. **"3-in-1 Female Sexual Wellness Blend" + "From Distant to Deeply Connected"**, both ✅ bible lines, as the standard lockup.
3. **Three icon callouts** around the product (ingredients in the originals; use ✅ benefits instead).
4. **Blush, pink and warm-beige light** for statics; **golden-hour sunset** for videos.
5. **Videos: a fixed info frame over full-bleed lifestyle footage.** The header, the three icons, the proof and "Shipped free from
   the pharmacy" stay on screen while the footage plays.
