# Category 3 — Cinematic Big Idea

Library folders: Movie-Peg · Wild-Creative/Surreal · Retro · New Year · Billboard (boards 1, 3, 4, 5, 6).
Why it works: a gentle pattern interrupt for a product people don't expect to see advertised; warm and tasteful, never suggestive.
(The team rejected surreal-from-scratch videos: keep it real, object-based, 720p.)

## 3A — Object metaphor
- **Looks like:** two coffee cups set down together on a sunlit counter; a door opening onto morning light; a silk ribbon untied; a rose
  opening in time-lapse. The bottle arrives in the final beat, label to camera. One Tier A line in finishing ("From Distant to Deeply Connected").
- **Guardrails:** the metaphor is about connection, never about bodies; nothing that reads as a bedroom.

## 3B — Gentle contrast
- **Looks like:** two chairs apart at a table, then side by side; one Tier A line; the product on the table.
- **Guardrails:** no couple shown; humour or warmth, never performance.

## 3C — Retro film
- **Looks like:** 70s film grain, a woman laughing with a friend at a café table (clothed, daytime), the product on the table.

## 3D — Seasonal
- **Looks like:** spring / anniversary / summer-evening framing with the product; no bedrooms, no lingerie, no gifts that imply intimacy.
