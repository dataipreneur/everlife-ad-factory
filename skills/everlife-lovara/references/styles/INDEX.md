## How to ask (minimal clicks)
1. **"Pick a style direction"** (single select): "✨ Recommend for me" (you pick 1–3 sub-styles with one line why) · "Product as Hero"
   (statics ~1 Creatify cr, video Seedance ~45 cr) · "Lifestyle & Real People" (Marketing Studio, ~75 cr/video) · "Authority & Big Idea".
2. **"Which one?"**: the sub-styles of that category (≤ 4 options, look + cost). Add "👀 Show me examples first" → Read the board, show it.
3. Batches: "Mix it up (different sub-style per concept)" is the default.

## Routing
| Need | Engine | Cost |

| Multi-scene, camera moves | Seedance 2.0 (+ bottle, tablet and cast still `--image`) | ~45 / 15 s (67.5 at 720p) |
| Statics | GPT Image 2.5 via Creatify, renders attached | ~1 Creatify credit |
| ALL words, numbers, logos, review text | finishing only (`finish_batch.py`, `full_layout.py`) | 0 |

## Library compliance traps (never copy)
"Breaking News" · beds, bedrooms, lingerie, undressing, couples, lips close-ups · body shots, before/after · "ED", "erectile", "Viagra",
"performance", "instant", timing numbers ("in 30 min") · "No More Needles" / needle imagery · celebrity names or look-alikes · $ prices ·
old proof (4.8 / 50,000 / 70,000) · named competitors · "Spark".
