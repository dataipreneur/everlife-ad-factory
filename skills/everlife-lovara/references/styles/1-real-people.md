# Category 1 — Real People Talking

Library folders: UGC · Talking Head Avatar · AI Avatar · Conversational · Podcast · Voice Over (boards 1, 2, 4, 6).
Why it works: the highest trust for a woman 30–55; a real face naming the product calmly. The talk stays about the product, never
about results, a partner or anyone's private life.

**Engine: Marketing Studio UGC** (`--mode ugc --product_ids @<skill>/assets/product_ids.json --duration 15 --resolution 720p --aspect_ratio 9:16 --generate-audio true`), 75 credits; needs Lovara registered. Never Seedance or still-image lip-sync for these.

Universal prompt lines: "ultra-realistic creator UGC, real handheld phone shake, real skin texture, real room audio, no beauty filter;
the filming phone is NEVER visible; label readable and never mirrored; fully clothed, daytime, head-and-shoulders framing; no bed or
bedroom in frame; an ordinary, non-famous face; nobody says 'I' or 'my'; keep the bottom 25% free of faces." The product-name line goes
over a hands-only shot and is re-voiced in the actor's cloned voice (model voices mangle names).

---

## 1A — Creator to camera
- **Looks like:** one woman 30–55 in a sunlit kitchen or on a terrace talking to her phone, the bottle by her cheek.
- **Beats:** 0–2.5 a pattern-interrupt action with the first words → 2.5–5 a ✅ territory line ("From distant to deeply connected.")
  → 5–8.5 product up (hands-only shot) + ONE ✅ line ("Supports desire and sensitivity.") → 8.5–10.5 "Doctor prescribed. Ships free from the pharmacy."
- **Finishing:** `tiktok`. **Guardrails:** describe the product, never her results or her partner.

## 1B — Friend-to-friend
- **Looks like:** two friends 35–55 at a café or on a walk; A asks what the small bottle is; B: "Lovara. Women's intimacy support. Doctor prescribed."
- **Guardrails:** Rx never shared; nothing about anyone's nights; the off-camera voice never testifies.
- **Finishing:** `default` (house look).
