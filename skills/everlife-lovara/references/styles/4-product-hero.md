# Category 4 — Product as Hero  (highest fit: the bible says "product imagery led")

Library folders: Product-as-Hero · Product Highlight · Pill-Product Focused · Minimalist · Polaroid (boards 3, 4, 5).
Why it works: tasteful by construction; the default for Lovara statics (Mitansh's premium-DTC direction from PT-141:
`core/references/static-refs/13_Premium_DTC/`).

## 4A — Soft still life
- **Looks like:** the bottle + one capsule on travertine or white linen, blush silk folds, soft daylight, a real shadow. Serif Tier A line.
- Static: batch F12. Video: Seedance slow push-in, 480p is enough.

## 4B — Capsule macro
- **Looks like:** a macro of the single capsule on linen or in an open palm, the bottle behind, label sharp. Never two capsules.
- Static: batch F2/F11. Video: Seedance macro orbit.

## 4C — Minimal bold
- **Looks like:** one product, one colour field (blush, sand, soft teal, warm white), one huge headline, up to three short lines.

## 4D — Ad in a frame
- **Looks like:** the ad as a framed print on a hallway wall or a postcard on a kitchen counter (never a nightstand).

## 4E — Getting-ready ASMR
- **Looks like:** hands set the bottle down on a bathroom counter among everyday objects (watch, keys, hand cream); a tap runs; a towel
  folds. Shoulder-up if a face appears; the mirror shows nothing below the shoulders.
- **Guardrails:** the product is placed, never taken on camera.
