# Which reference to use when (Lovara)

Order (`core/references/REFERENCE-PRIORITY.md`): **1** our own Lovara winners (none on file yet: ask the user for the Winning Ads
Reference rows or a Drive download) → **2** Meta Ad Library refs in `core/references/meta-refs/` → **3** the shared Pinterest library
`core/references/static-refs/` (12 folders; overview `OVERVIEW.jpg`). It teaches **layout, type scale, product staging and mood**, never
words or scenes. Pick ONE pin per slot, look at it, and borrow only its structure.

**PMG Ad Drive Lovara samples** ("Horny in 30 min", bedroom and lips shots) break the rules: layout reference only, never their words,
timing claims or scenes.

| Lovara angle (bible) | Best frameworks | In the folder, pick the pin that is… | Avoid in that folder |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Connection** — "From Distant to Deeply Connected", "Unlock Confidence, Desire & Connection" | F2 Bold Claim · F13 Portrait + line · F11 Visual Metaphor | 02: big-type, one-idea pins over calm space, product lower third; 12: candid person + product pins (framing only); 11: single-object metaphor pins | anything with a bed, a couple or lingerie |
| **The 3-in-1 blend** — "3-in-1 Female Sexual Wellness Blend" | F4 Features & Benefits · F9 Reasons Why (advertorial on white) | 04: three-icon-row pins with ONE product and lots of air; 09: the numbered "3 reasons" pins | 04's neon/3D pins |
| **Say yes to more** — "Say Yes to More Pleasure, Desire & Intimacy" (with care) | F12 Brand Mood · F2 · F6 Offer | 12: blush/cream still lifes; 06: clean percent-off pins (never $) | anything suggestive |
| **Trust** (doctor prescribed, pharmacy, 100,000+) | F2 · F6 · F12 | 06 percent-off; 12 pharmacy-feel still lifes | 07 until reviews exist |

Rules of thumb:
- One promise per ad and one plain line that explains it.
- If a pin's mood is "bedroom", take only its type scale and leave the scene.
- Never reuse the same pin for two slots in one batch; never the same framework twice unless the batch is bigger than 8.
- Colours: pinks, tans, warm neutrals, white, teal (`image-batch.md` §2b).
