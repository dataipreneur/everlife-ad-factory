# Category 1 — Real People Talking

Library folders: UGC · Talking Head Avatar · AI Avatar · Conversational · Podcast · Voice Over (boards 1, 2, 4, 6).
Why it works: the highest trust for a 35–60 buyer; a real face naming the goal out loud.

**Engine: Marketing Studio UGC** (`--mode ugc --product_ids @<skill>/assets/product_ids.json --duration 15 --resolution 720p --aspect_ratio 9:16 --generate-audio true`), 75 credits. Never Seedance or still-image lip-sync for these.

Universal prompt lines: "ultra-realistic creator UGC, real handheld phone shake, real skin texture, real room audio, no beauty filter; the filming phone is NEVER visible; exactly ONE tablet; label readable and never mirrored; fully clothed, natural build, not a bodybuilder; nobody says 'I' or 'my'; say 'Sermorelin GHK' as 'ser-mo-REL-in G-H-K'; keep the bottom 25% free of faces."

---

## 1A — Creator to camera
- **Looks like:** one person (men 45–60 in a garage gym or truck after training; women 40–60 in a sunlit kitchen) talking to their phone, bottle by the cheek.
- **Beats:** 0–2 a pattern-interrupt action (sets down a heavy dumbbell mid-sentence) → 2–6 the goal as a pronoun-free question ("Recovery taking longer these days?") → 6–10 bottle up + ONE mechanism line ("Sermorelin stimulates natural growth hormone production") → 10–12 the tablet to the lens.
- **Finishing:** `tiktok`. **Guardrails:** describe the product, never their results.

## 1B — Training partner POV
- **Looks like:** a training partner films him/her between sets, teasing; the person holds up their OWN bottle from the gym bag.
- **Guardrails:** Rx never shared; the off-camera voice never testifies ("he's jacked now" ✗).
- **Finishing:** `tiktok` or `editorial`.

## 1C — Friends chatting
- **Looks like:** two friends 50+ on a trail or a café terrace after a run; A asks what's different; B describes the product: "3-in-1. Sermorelin, GHK, Citrulline. Easy sublingual delivery."
- **Finishing:** `house`.

## 1D — Podcast set
- **Looks like:** two hosts at a warm podcast desk, the bottle and tablet on the desk.
- **Beats:** the host's hot take ("Nobody talks about growth hormone after 40") → each host gives ONE ingredient + its own ✅ claim → product on desk.
- **Finishing:** `editorial`. **Guardrails:** no "studies show" for the product.

## 1E — Voice-over lifestyle
- **Looks like:** a calm narrator over 3–4 candid shots of one person's morning (training, work, family), benefit chips added in finishing.
- **Finishing:** `house` or `editorial`.
