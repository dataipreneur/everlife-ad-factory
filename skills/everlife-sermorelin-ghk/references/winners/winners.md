## How to use these cards (read before writing a batch)
- Take **layout, format, hook mechanics, mood and product staging** from a card. Take **every word** from the bible
  (a bible reference line) or as an approved a fresh line line. The image model never sees these ads, only you do.
- **Never copy the "Don't copy" items.** Most winners predate the 11 Sep 2026 rules or break them, e.g. "GHK-Cu", fat
  claims, "Save 70% vs HGH", wrong ratings, made-up ingredient pills. A winner's headline that isn't in the bible is a fresh line.
- Small samples (3 purchases) can be luck; weight the higher-volume winners (M8, M5, F4, F5).

## What our winners have in common
1. **Product name huge**, usually as the headline or a banner ("SERMORELIN GHK" in heavy condensed type).
2. **The real green "S" tablet, big and close to the viewer**, beside the bottle (tablet hero).
3. **"3-in-1" made visual**: three ingredient tiles or columns joined by "+" (Sermorelin + GHK + Citrulline).
4. **Short power headlines** ("Lean. Strong. Ready.", "Stay toned. Get lean.") for men; **glow + strength** for women.
5. **Proof footer** (Trustpilot badge, Doctor Prescribed, Ships Free From Pharmacy) on almost all statics.
6. **Gym / training context for men** (dumbbells, dark gym); **warm, soft, skin-care light for women**.
7. Formats that win beyond the bold claim: **offer badge**, **"Guys, after 35" identity post**, **open letter**, **article**, **UGC creator video**.
8. None of them mention strength, dose, "double strength" or customer reviews.
